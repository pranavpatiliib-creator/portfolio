PROFILE IMAGE PLACEHOLDER
=========================

Replace this folder's profile.jpg with your actual photo.

Steps:
1. Copy your photo into this folder and name it:   profile.jpg
2. Recommended size: 300×300 px or larger, square crop (JPEG or PNG)
3. The portfolio hero section will automatically display it with a
   circular crop, glowing border, and hover effect.

If no profile.jpg is found, the website shows an avatar generated from
your name via ui-avatars.com (configured in the img onerror handler).

FAVICON
=======
Add a favicon.png (32×32 px) to this folder and link it in index.html:
  <link rel="icon" type="image/png" href="assets/favicon.png" />
